The Challenge

Millions of graduates.
Not enough opportunities.

Every year, hundreds of thousands of Nigerian students graduate and enter a job market that is not ready for them. Universities produce brilliant minds, but employers report a persistent gap in practical skills, workplace readiness, and professional networks.
